Cinematic photorealistic documentary reenactment still, shot on 35mm film with an anamorphic lens. Color grade inspired by True Detective season 1, Se7en and Prisoners: desaturated palette, cool shadows pushed toward steel blue and teal, neutral-cool midtones, slight bleach-bypass contrast, deep rich blacks, soft atmospheric haze, subtle film grain. One single small warm amber light source as the focal accent (headlights, a lit window, a glow on the summit); everything else cold. Moody, tense, investigative. People only as silhouettes or seen from behind, faces never visible. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks anywhere. 16:9 widescreen.
